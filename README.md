
# **MY FAVORITE MOVIE**

What is The Maze Runner?
 
- The Maze Runner is a dystopian science fiction movie released in 2014, based on the novel by James Dashner. Directed by Wes Ball, starring Dylan O'Brien as Thomas.

- The story starts when Thomas wakes up in a metal elevator called the Box with no memory, only his name. He arrives in the Glade — a large open area in the center of a giant, moving maze. There are 50+ boys living there, they call themselves Gladers. Every morning the walls of the maze open, and at night they close. No one has ever survived a night in the maze because of the Grievers — monster machines.

- Their supplies come from the Box every month. Then one day, a girl named Teresa arrives and says everything is about to change. Thomas and the Gladers discover that the maze is a test made by an organization called WICKED, and they have to solve it to escape and find out the truth about the outside world.
 
What did I like about this Movie
 
- What I liked about this movie is the action and mystery. From the start, you want to know what is outside the maze and why the boys are trapped there.

- I liked that it's not boring — every night the maze changes, so you feel the tension. The friendship and teamwork of the Gladers is also good, they have different jobs like Runners, Builders, and Med-jacks. The twist at the end is also surprising, when they find out they were part of an experiment.

- The visuals are also good, especially the giant walls and the Grievers.
 
Favorite Character to this Movie
 
- My favorite character is Thomas.

- I like him because he is brave and curious. Even though he is new, he is not afraid to enter the maze, unlike the others who followed the rules for 2 years. He is a leader and he thinks about everyone, not just himself. He is the reason why the Gladers had hope to escape.

- My second favorite is Minho

- He is the fastest Runner and funny, he helps Thomas a lot in the maze. And Chuck — he is kind and like a younger brother to Thomas.
